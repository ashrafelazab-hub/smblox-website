How it works

  1. We measure first

    A Perception Audit of your website, LinkedIn, AI-search answers, third-party footprint, peers and visuals. You get a baseline score and every gap, ranked.

  2. We fix the foundation

    Positioning, proof and the pages buyers judge you on. We don't amplify a weak message.

  3. The system runs every week

    Agents draft posts, articles and outreach from real signals. A separate checker reviews every draft before you see it. You approve in about 30 minutes.

  4. You see the results every month

    One report: what we delivered, what moved, what failed and what we change next. At day 90 we audit again and show the change.

Questions

What is SMBlox?

SMBlox runs the marketing function of an early-stage cybersecurity company with AI agents directed by security business judgement, and gives its sales team AI tools to find and prepare the right conversations. You approve; the system does the work.

Who is SMBlox for?

Cybersecurity companies with roughly 5 to 100 people and at least one real customer: startup vendors with one or two products, and service providers or integrators. We start with companies selling in the UAE.

Do you need access to our security systems?

No, never. We work from public information, your answers and the marketing accounts you choose to share, such as your LinkedIn page, at the lowest access level that does the job.

How is this different from a marketing agency?

We work only with security companies, we measure how buyers and AI search see you before we change anything, and every output rests on evidence. We do not send volume or publish content without a business reason.

Do you guarantee a number of meetings?

No. We set targets with you after four weeks of real data, and every monthly report shows what worked and what did not.

How much of our time does it take?

About 30 minutes a week to approve drafts, plus 15 minutes from your expert to answer five short questions, so what we publish carries your real knowledge.
